ABOUT ME

Exploring radio waves is also a way of exploring the world.

I am Héctor Quintas Iglesias, an author, amateur radio operator and photographer, known in the radio community by my callsign CE4RWS.

Amateur radio has allowed me to explore radio waves, study propagation, discover distant places and understand a form of communication capable of connecting people thousands of kilometres apart.

A place to share knowledge

Writing is another way to explore and share what I have learned over the years. My books bring together knowledge, experience and research related mainly to amateur radio, antennas, radio-wave propagation, radio history and technology.

Photography is also a way of observing the world, stopping time for an instant and preserving places and moments that tell a story beyond the image.
